+====================================================================+
|         SMART ENERGY DASHBOARD — HOW TO START (30 seconds)         |
+====================================================================+

FOR THE OPERATOR — NO TECH NEEDED

1. LOOK AT YOUR DESKTOP — find the icon named:
      Smart Energy Dashboard
   It has a blue power-plant icon (from PowerStudio).

2. DOUBLE-CLICK that icon — just double-click once.

3. WAIT 5 SECONDS — the dashboard will open by itself in your
   web browser (Chrome/Edge). You will see:
      - Top: PLANT TOTAL kW / kWh / PF + system status
      - Middle: 8 METER CARDS (Main, Floor1, DG...) with live numbers
      - Click MC tab to see all MC variables from PowerStudio

4. DONE! Leave the browser open. The dashboard updates every 3 seconds
   automatically. You do NOT need to press anything else.

   If the browser did not open:
     -> Double-click the second icon:  Dashboard (browser only)
     -> Or open Chrome and type:  http://127.0.0.1:8000

5. TO CLOSE: just close the browser. The dashboard keeps running
   in background and will start again when you restart the PC
   (it is in Startup — autostart).
